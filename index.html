Independent Advisory for Shipowners Docking in China

Dry docking in China can offer competitive pricing, but shipowners often face hidden cost escalation, variation claims and communication challenges with yards.

Zhoushan Eastern Docking Advisory works exclusively on the owner's side, providing independent commercial insight based on real shipyard operational experience.

Our objective is simple: protect owner interests and control docking costs.

The founder previously worked for many years in the commercial departments of major Chinese shipyards and has direct experience with dry docking negotiations, cost structures and operational challenges.

Today the firm works independently to help shipowners reduce cost, avoid commercial risks and improve communication efficiency when dealing with Chinese shipyards.
